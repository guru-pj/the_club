
🏃‍♂️ The Club – Feature List (Day 1 Complete)

MVP (Phase 1)
--------------
☐ User signup/login (JWT-secured)
☐ Create/join clubs
☐ Log activities (manual entry)
☐ Club feed (basic posts from owners)
☐ Simple club leaderboard (distance, time, sessions)

Extended MVP (Phase 2+)
------------------------
☐ Club owners create events (e.g., weekend runs)
☐ Set registration opening time in-app
☐ Users register inside the app
☐ In-app payment for events
☐ Confirmation message + email + QR code ticket
☐ Club owner scans QR code on event day for check-in
☐ Event posts with photos + recap
☐ Strava integration: only club owner’s activity linked to posts
☐ Individual members’ Strava activities stored in their account
☐ Track member participation count in club events
☐ Award points/stars for participation
☐ Monthly leaderboard: top individuals + top clubs

Future Features (Later Roadmap)
-------------------------------
☐ Cross-club competitions
☐ Club-based training plans
☐ Full Strava sync (personal activities + auto-linking to events)
☐ In-app chat/forum per club
☐ Mobile notifications & reminders
☐ Club membership subscriptions

💡 Suggestions (to review later)
--------------------------------
☐ Gym groups with shared training sessions
☐ Club-based workout logging (gym, yoga, swimming, etc.)
☐ Shared training plans inside a club (coach creates → members follow)
☐ Club-level leaderboards for strength workouts (e.g., most sessions logged)
☐ Event-specific medals or digital badges
☐ City or regional ranking of clubs
☐ Integration with wearables (Garmin, Apple Watch, etc.)
